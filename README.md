## 🌍 About the Project
**GyanSetu** (*The Bridge of Knowledge*) is an independent, student-driven digital internet radio and knowledge platform created for competition. 

Its defining purpose is **universal knowledge dissemination**: explaining difficult, intimidating concepts in an intuitive, crystal-clear, and captivating way for any curious learner on Earth—without requiring apps, subscriptions, or paywalls.

The scope of GyanSetu extends beyond conventional science into deep philosophy, the mystery of human consciousness, everyday curiosities, modern technological frontiers, and the history of breakthrough ideas.

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Apple Liquid Glass Design System
The visual architecture is derived directly from the official GyanSetu brand emblem:
* **Deep Cosmic Navy (`#071322`)**: Midnight foundation ensuring deep contrast and optical immersion.
* **Sage Teal (`#509B93` / `#65A8A0`)**: Primary lighting and accent tones sampled directly from the logo canvas.
* **Tangerine Accent (`#F97C22` / `#FFA048`)**: Energetic focal highlight honoring the logo's typography and wave motif.
* **Apple Liquid Glass Materials**:
  * Multi-layer frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(40px) saturate(190%)`).
  * Specular top hairline sheen borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)`).
  * Continuous organic curvature (`border-radius: 28px` cards and `9999px` floating navigation).
  * Floating Liquid Glass audio dock with live waveform visualizers and tactile controls.
